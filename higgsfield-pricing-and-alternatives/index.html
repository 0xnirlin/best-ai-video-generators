Higgsfield pricing and alternatives for micro dramas and anime

Higgsfield is a popular AI video app. This page lists what its plans cost, what a 5 second clip costs on each, and what to use when the job is a whole micro drama or anime episode and not a single clip.

Higgsfield figures are as its pricing page read on 14 October 2026. Its sign-up bonus was seen on 3 October 2026. Prices change often, so check higgsfield.ai/pricing before you buy.

Is Higgsfield free?

Not as a plan. When its pricing page was read on 14 October 2026, Basic, Pro and Max were all paid, and the cheapest, Basic, was $9 a month for 120 credits.

What the page did list for free was a handful of generations on specific tools: 10 free generations each on Higgsfield AI Influencer and Ads Studio on Basic, and up to 30 on AI Influencer on Pro and Max. On 3 October 2026 the sign-up screen offered 50 credits for signing in with a business email. That was not re-checked and may have changed.

Pro and Max also listed a 7-day unlimited trial of Wan 3.0, Nano Banana 2 and Kling 3.0 (Max adds Nano Banana Pro). The page notes that unlimited usage may slow at busy times.

A clip is not an episode

Take one episode of 60 seconds: 12 shots of 5 seconds at 768P, 6 reference images for the cast, 8 spoken lines, one score cue and 5 planning chats.

The 12 clips alone cost about $6.30 on Higgsfield Basic (Kling 3.0 at 720p, about 7 credits each, monthly billing). That is the clips only. The rest of the job is still to do: the script, the shot list, a cast that looks the same in every shot, the voices, the score and the cut. Each is another tool or another hour, and each handoff is a chance for a character to change face.

The pick for micro dramas and anime Recommended: Ziztu

If the job is an episode where the same characters appear in every shot, the pick is Ziztu. It is an AI video studio for micro dramas and anime, built to take one idea to a finished cut on one credit balance.

  • One idea in, a planned cut out. A Director turns an idea or a script into a shot list. Character sheets keep the cast the same from shot to shot. Clips, music, voice and captions sit on one timeline.
  • Many models, one balance. H3 Max Turbo, Kling, Seedance and Grok video, plus several image models, draw on one pool of credits, so each shot can use the model that suits it.
  • The price shows in credits before you run. A 5 second H3 Max Turbo clip is 310 credits at 480P and 500 credits at 768P.
  • The whole episode priced. The 60 second example above comes to 7,750 credits on Ziztu, covering the shots, cast images, voices, score and planning chats on one balance, with the same characters in every shot. Retakes cost extra. This is a worked example from the rate card, not a quote.

To be fair about clips alone: Kling 3.0 at 720p is cheaper on Higgsfield than the Kling model Ziztu runs, though they are not the same Kling model, and credits are not the same unit across tools. Compare finished clips and finished episodes, not credit counts.
