## How to choose

- **Daily Shorts:** a reel maker. Reel Factory if you want ready styles, word captions and music in one run; faceless.video or faceless.so if scheduled posting matters most.
- **One explainer a week:** Pictory or Invideo AI, built around editing from a script.
- **A 20 to 40 minute documentary:** the AI YouTube Video Maker, since most of the others list a cap at or below 40 minutes.
- **A presenter without being on camera:** HeyGen, with the avatar caveat.

## What YouTube says (read 25 October 2026)

- **Monetization:** YouTube's [channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392) cover inauthentic content, meaning mass-produced or repetitive videos. The same page says you may use AI to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented. Give every video your own angle and checked facts.
- **Disclosure:** YouTube asks you to [label realistic AI content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491), such as a realistic scene that did not happen. Clearly unreal content such as animation does not need the label.
- **No promises:** no tool can promise views or monetization. That is YouTube's decision.
